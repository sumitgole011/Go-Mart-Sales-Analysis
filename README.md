# Go Mart Sales Analysis | Interactive Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

The objective of the **Go Mart Sales Analysis** project is to analyze the store's annual sales data for 2022 and develop an interactive dashboard using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard helps identify customer purchasing patterns, sales trends, and high-performing product categories and sales channels to support better business decisions for future growth.

## Dataset

- **Dataset Source:** [Excel Sales Dataset](https://github.com/ritikbh193/Data-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/main/Vrinda%20Data%20Analysis2.xlsx)
- **Reporting Period:** January–December 2022
- **Tool Used:** Microsoft Excel

## Key Business Questions (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on the following business questions:

- How do monthly sales revenue and order volumes compare?
- Which months recorded the highest sales and order counts?
- What is the purchasing distribution between male and female customers?
- What is the breakdown of orders by delivery and other order statuses?
- Which 10 states generated the highest sales?
- How do customer age groups and gender influence purchasing patterns?
- Which sales channels generate the most revenue?
- Which product categories perform best?
- What percentage of orders were successfully delivered?

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning:** Checked the dataset for missing values, duplicates, inconsistent entries, and data anomalies.
2. **Data Preparation:** Standardized data formats, corrected data types where required, and organized the fields for analysis.
3. **Exploratory Analysis:** Created PivotTables to summarize sales, order counts, customer demographics, locations, categories, and sales channels.
4. **Dashboard Development:** Combined the visualizations into a unified Excel dashboard for easier interpretation.
5. **Interactive Filtering:** Added slicers to allow users to explore the data using available filters and compare different customer and sales segments.

## Key Insights

Based on the analysis of the supplied dataset:

- **Customer Gender:** Female customers accounted for approximately 65% of purchases, indicating a higher share of orders than male customers.
- **Top-performing States:** Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh were among the leading states in sales contribution.
- **Customer Age Groups:** Customers aged 30–49 represented approximately 50% of purchases, making this an important customer segment.
- **Sales Channels:** Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra were among the major contributors to order volume.
- **Order Fulfilment:** More than 90% of orders were delivered, suggesting a high delivery completion rate.

## Business Recommendations

To improve Go Mart's sales performance, the business should prioritize marketing campaigns for customers aged 30–49, with particular attention to high-performing states such as Maharashtra, Karnataka, and Uttar Pradesh.

Additional recommendations include:

- Develop targeted promotions based on customer demographics and purchasing behaviour.
- Strengthen sales strategies on high-performing online marketplaces.
- Analyze monthly sales trends to plan seasonal offers and inventory levels.
- Review undelivered and cancelled orders to identify potential fulfilment issues.
- Monitor product category performance to improve product availability and promotional planning.

## Conclusion

The **Go Mart Sales Analysis Dashboard** transforms raw sales data into actionable business insights using Microsoft Excel. By examining customer demographics, monthly sales trends, state-wise performance, product categories, and sales channels, the dashboard supports data-driven decisions aimed at improving customer engagement and increasing future sales.
